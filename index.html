Spaced Out

Camp NASA 2025

June 5-7

NASA's Space Camp for High Schoolers

Welcome to NASA's 2025 Space Camp: Spaced Out, a three-day exploration of the beauty and mystery of space. This year’s program focuses on the wonder of the cosmos, combining hands-on astronaut training with activities while also exploring celestial navigation, cosmic photography, and guided stargazing sessions with NASA experts. Through interactive workshops and immersive simulations, students will discover the science behind the stars and the mysteries that make space so captivating. It’s an opportunity to step beyond Earth and see space in a whole new way.
